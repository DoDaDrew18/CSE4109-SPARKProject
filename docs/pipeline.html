SPARK · County-Level Respiratory Nowcasting Pipeline

Can LLM-extracted facts from local news improve county nowcasts of the % of ED visits that are respiratory when the model sees only the delayed, still-revising official numbers? Every box lists what goes in and what comes out.

The one rule · no time travel Every feature row for prediction date D is built only from what was published on or before D: official data through load_vintage(source, D), news only if published ≤ D. Labels come from the final (latest) vintage and are used only for scoring. Red ⛔ boxes mark where this is enforced.

Why vintages · one county-week, many truths

Same reference week, published again and again. Models must use the value on the date they predict, not the final one. At the Jan 2026 flu peak, 19% of counties' first prints were revised by ≥10%.
